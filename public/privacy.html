Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Chikichiki Studios ("the Service") is a small publishing tool that lets a creator publish their own original videos to their own TikTok account, either immediately from the posting page or on a schedule they set up themselves. This policy explains what the Service stores and why.

Who we are

Chikichiki Studios is operated by an individual developer. You can reach us at kamitikitiki@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens. When you connect your TikTok account, TikTok issues tokens that let the Service post on your behalf. These are stored encrypted at rest by our hosting provider and are never shown in your browser.
  • Your TikTok user identifier (open_id), so the Service knows which account the tokens belong to.
  • A session cookie, which contains only that identifier in signed form. It is strictly necessary for the Service to work and is not used for analytics or advertising.

What we do not collect

  • We do not store the videos you upload. A file is held in memory only for the duration of the upload to TikTok and is not retained afterwards.
  • We do not collect your TikTok password. Authentication happens entirely on TikTok.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party cookies.
  • We do not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages or comments.

Information received from TikTok

When you open the posting page, the Service asks TikTok for your display name, avatar and current posting settings, so it can show you which account you are posting to and which options are available. This information is displayed to you and is not stored.

How the information is used

Only to publish videos you have explicitly chosen to publish. There are two ways to do that:

  • Immediately, from the posting page: you select a video, choose a privacy setting and press the Post button.
  • On a schedule: you place a video you have approved into a queue you control, and a scheduled job that you operate publishes it at the time you configured, using the caption and privacy settings you configured for that queue.

In both cases the video, the decision to publish it and its settings come from you. The Service never selects, alters or publishes content on its own initiative.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing or any other purpose. The following providers process data solely to operate the Service:

  • TikTok receives the video and its settings in order to publish it.
  • Cloudflare hosts the Service and stores the tokens described above.
  • GitHub runs the scheduled-publishing job, if you use one. That job receives a short-lived access token (valid for at most 24 hours) for each run; it never receives the refresh token.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept for as long as your account is connected. "Disconnect" on the posting page signs this browser out; it does not by itself revoke the Service's access, so that any scheduled publishing you have set up keeps working. To revoke access, use Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions inside the TikTok app, which immediately invalidates the tokens. To have the stored record deleted as well, email us at the address above and we will remove it.

Children

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age required to hold a TikTok account in their country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
